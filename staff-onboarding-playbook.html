Staff Onboarding Playbook

Onboard new staff in 48 hours, not 48 days

What This Is

A 48-hour onboarding system that covers all critical operations in the first two days: company culture, technical tools, workflows, reporting, escalation, and team dynamics. No training videos, no docs left unread, no questions unanswered.

48-Hour Schedule

Day 1 (Day A)

  • 09:00-10:00: Welcome & orientation (org chart, core values, communication style)
  • 10:30-12:00: Technical tools (Git, project management, reporting tools)
  • 13:00-14:30: First workflow walkthrough (core tasks, deadlines, acceptance criteria)
  • 15:00-16:00: Team introductions, Q&A

Day 2 (Day B)

  • 09:00-10:30: Escalation & incident response (what to do when things break)
  • 11:00-12:00: Second workflow walkthrough (advanced tasks, patterns, common pitfalls)
  • 13:00-14:30: Performance review setup (KPIs, OKRs, 90-day check-in)
  • 15:00-16:00: Final review, next steps, offboarding check

What this doesn't cover: Legal contracts, payroll setup, benefits packages. For those, consult your HR provider.

Apply This

Questions

Q: What if my onboarding takes longer than 48 hours?
A: This is a baseline. Adjust based on your organization's needs. The structure is the same: cover culture, tools, workflows, escalation, and performance.

Q: What if I have multiple teams?
A: Use the template for each team. Teams have different workflows, tools, and cultures. Adjust accordingly.

Q: What if I have remote or hybrid staff?
A: Use video for sessions, share docs online, use tools like Slack/Teams for check-ins. The structure is the same.

Q: Can I customize this?
A: Yes. This is a template. Fill in your organization's details, adjust the schedule, add/remove sections based on your needs.
