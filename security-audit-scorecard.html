Reading your score

  • 80–90 (green): you are in the top band of small teams. Keep the twice-a-year cadence and batch the remaining hygiene.
  • 55–79 (amber): normal. Sequence the unchecked items by zone order — identity first; the 45-point walkthrough gives you the evidence rule and the day schedule.
  • Under 55 (red): the estate is running on luck. Do not buy tooling yet — close the identity and backup gaps first; they are a week of focused work.

The zone-4 flag: if Data & backups scores under 10 of 16, treat it as the critical gap regardless of your total. An untested backup is the difference between a bad Tuesday and an extinction event.
