How to Reduce Your Incident Costs

1. Build a Runbook Library

Document your top 5 incident types with step-by-step remediation. A junior engineer following a runbook can resolve incidents 3x faster than one improvising.

2. Automate Alert Routing

Every alert should create a ticket with context — not just a page. The on-call engineer should know what broke, where to look, and what to try first.

3. Practice Incident Response

Run a "game day" once a quarter. Simulate an incident and have your team respond. You'll find gaps in your runbooks before a real incident does.

4. Use Communication Templates

Don't write status updates from scratch during an incident. Pre-write templates for SEV1, SEV2, and SEV3 communications.

5. Postmortem Every Incident

Every incident is a learning opportunity. Write a postmortem within 24 hours. Identify root cause, action items, and owners.
