Verdict: Which Tool Should You Choose?

For Enterprise Teams (50+ engineers): PagerDuty

PagerDuty is the industry standard for large organizations. If you have 50+ engineers, complex escalation policies, and budget for $21-$41/user/month, it's the right choice.

For Mid-Size Teams (10-50 engineers): Opsgenie

Opsgenie (by Atlassian) integrates well with Jira and Confluence. At $9-$17/user/month, it's more affordable than PagerDuty for mid-size teams.

For Startups (5-10 engineers): Better Stack

Better Stack combines monitoring + on-call in one tool. Good for startups that want simplicity. $20-$50/month.

For Small Teams (1-5 engineers): Hive80 Ops Kit

For teams of 1-5 engineers, the big tools are overkill. You don't need complex scheduling — you need runbooks, templates, and a clear process. That's what Hive80 provides:
