Why This Works

  1. Labor cost % - Your #1 control lever. Over 35% = losing money.
  2. Food cost % - Your #2 lever. Over 35% = check portions and waste.
  3. Daily revenue vs. target - Under target 3 days = something needs to change.
Common mistake: Filling out the report but not acting on it. Every day, pick ONE thing to improve tomorrow.
