Email is the #1 attack vector. The Email Security Checklist covers the 7 controls that stop 95% of email-borne attacks: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, 2FA, password manager, phishing training, and forwarding-rule audits. Each takes under an hour. Pair it with the Security Audit Checklist for a full assessment, and the Ransomware Recovery Checklist for when prevention fails.

3. Vendor management (start with the Vendor Security Review)

Your vendors are your attack surface. The Vendor Security Review Checklist covers onboarding, risk assessment, and the monthly review.
