  • Retainer Renewal Checklist for Small Teams (The Easiest Sale You'll Ever Make)
    The easiest sale is the one already won: the 60-day renewal runway with four fixed stops, the one-page value memo (three outcome numbers and the prevented list, not a slide deck), three renewal paths decided in week one (renew as-is, rescope, graceful offboard), the pause-not-lapse clause that turns cancellations into deferrals, the fifteen-minute renewal call that is a yes/no on the memo instead of a negotiation, and the price increase that rides the renewal with options instead of ambush. Worked example: 14-client agency, 12 of 14 renewed, one rescope added $18k ARR, renewal admin down from 6 hours to 2 per client.
  • No-Show Reminder Sequence for Client Appointments (Templates That Fill the Calendar)
    The 3-touch reminder ladder that makes clients show up: the 48-hour confirm (one-word reply-bait), the 24-hour commitment text, the 2-hour logistics text; the reply-to-confirm mechanic that turns reminders into attendance forecasts, the no-show policy written once with its forgiveness clause, the 15-minute same-day rescue script, the two-strike prepay rule for repeat offenders, and five weekly numbers led by confirm rate. Worked example: two-room physio clinic, 13 empty beds a week down to under 4, Fridays back to 85% booked, five hours of reminder calls a week deleted.
  • Upfront Deposit Checklist for Small Teams (Never Start Work Unfunded)
    The quote is an agreement; the deposit is the evidence: the unfunded-work trap and the math of one unpaid cancellation erasing several good jobs, the 50/50 rule with its shape exceptions (100% under ~$1,500, 40/40/20 for phased, first month up front on retainers), the payment schedule written onto the quote face with the card link beside it ("reserves your slot"), the two-sentence script for resisters with milestone-split and escrow trust substitutes, the earned-deposit cancellation line, and five weekly numbers led by unfunded kickoffs. Worked example: design studio, $11,400 of donated hours a year, deposit rate 100% inside a quarter, time-to-first-payment 47 days to 3.
  • Change Order & Scope Creep Checklist for Small Teams (Stop Working for Free)
