Small Business Security Audit: 12-Point Checklist

Updated 2026 - 15 min read - Run it in under 1 hour

#Security#Cybersecurity#SmallBusiness#Compliance

Most small businesses get hacked not because of sophisticated attacks, but because of basic security gaps they didn't know they had.

Stat: 43% of cyberattacks target small businesses. 60% go out of business within 6 months of a major breach.

1. Credential Management

Are you using a password manager?

  • Every employee has a password manager
  • No passwords shared via email or Slack
  • Default passwords on all devices changed
  • Service accounts use generated passwords

2. Access Controls

Does each person have minimum access?

  • Access is role-based, not person-based
  • Departed employees' access revoked within 24h
  • Admin access limited to those who need it
  • Quarterly access review process

3. Data Classification

Do you know what data you have?

  • Data classified: Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted
  • Restricted data encrypted at rest
  • You know where all data lives
  • Data retention policy exists

4. Incident Response Plan

If hacked right now, what would you do?

  • Written incident response plan exists
  • Plan includes who to call
  • Plan has been tested
  • Everyone knows where to find it

5. Backup Verification

Could you recover from ransomware?

  • Backups automated and daily
  • Backups stored offline/separate
  • Tested a restore in last 6 months
  • Retention covers 30+ days

6. Patch Management

Are systems up to date?

  • OS updates within 30 days
  • App updates within 14 days
  • Security patches within 72 hours
  • Software inventory maintained

7-12: Network Segmentation, Phishing Awareness, Device Management, Third-Party Risk, Monitoring, Governance

Points 7-12 cover: Network segmentation (guest WiFi separation, VLANs), Phishing awareness (training, simulations, SPF/DKIM/DMARC), Device management (encryption, remote wipe, MDM), Third-party risk (vendor reviews, data processing agreements), Monitoring (login alerts, anomaly detection, log retention), and Governance (security owner, policies, annual audits).

Scoring

  • 48/48: Excellent. Ahead of 95% of small businesses.
  • 36-47: Good. Fix the gaps.
  • 24-35: At risk. Prioritize immediately.
  • Below 24: Critical. Fix this today.
