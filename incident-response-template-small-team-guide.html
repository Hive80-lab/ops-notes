The First 30 Minutes Checklist

What you do in the first 30 minutes determines whether an incident is a minor issue or a major disaster.

🚨 First 30 Minutes - Critical Actions

DON'T PANIC - Take 30 seconds to breathe. Panic leads to mistakes.
DOCUMENT EVERYTHING - Screenshots, timestamps, error messages. This evidence is gold later.
ISOLATE THE PROBLEM - Unplug affected machines from the network if you can't identify the source.
PRESERVE EVIDENCE - Don't reboot servers or delete logs. Take memory dumps if possible.
COMMUNICATE - Let stakeholders know there's an incident. Be honest about what you know.
START THE LOGBOOK - Document times, actions, who you contacted, what you found.

Incident Response Phases

Small teams need a simplified incident response process. Here are the key phases:

Phase 1: Detection & Analysis

Identify that something is wrong and assess the scope. Don't jump to conclusions - gather facts first.

Phase 2: Containment

Stop the bleeding. Isolate affected systems, block malicious IPs, change compromised passwords.

Phase 3: Eradication

Remove the threat. Delete malware, patch vulnerabilities, clean infected systems.

Phase 4: Recovery

Restore systems from clean backups. Verify everything is working before going live.

Phase 5: Post-Mortem

Learn from the incident. What went well? What didn't? How can we prevent this?
